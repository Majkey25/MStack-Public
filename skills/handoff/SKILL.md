# Handoff

Give the next worker enough verified context to resume at the first unresolved action.

- Record the goal, accepted constraints, current artifact or revision, and the most important decisions with reasons.
- List completed changes and checks with results. Include exact pending checks, blockers, required access, and the next command or file to open when confirmed.
- Mark assumptions, uncommitted changes, and the distinction between observed and expected outcomes. Point to durable evidence rather than pasting a long transcript.

Use an authorized destination. Keep credentials, account data, and unrelated conversations out of the handoff. For external recipients, include only material approved for that audience.
